Cyber Threat Knowledge Base

Comprehensive information about cyber threats and prevention strategies

Types of Cyberattacks

  • Phishing: Deceptive attempts to steal sensitive information
  • Ransomware: Malicious software that blocks access until ransom is paid
  • DDoS Attacks: Overwhelming systems with traffic to disrupt services
  • Malware: Malicious software designed to damage or gain unauthorized access
  • Man-in-the-Middle: Intercepting communications between two parties

Common Vulnerabilities

  • Weak Passwords: Easily guessable or reused passwords
  • Unpatched Software: Outdated systems with security holes
  • Lack of Encryption: Unprotected data transmission
  • Social Engineering: Manipulating people into revealing information
  • Insufficient Access Controls: Poor permission management

Prevention Tips

  • Use strong, unique passwords for all accounts
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)
  • Keep software and systems updated
  • Regularly backup important data
  • Educate employees about security best practices
  • Implement network security monitoring

Case Studies

  • WannaCry (2017): Global ransomware attack affecting 150+ countries
  • SolarWinds (2020): Sophisticated supply chain attack
  • Colonial Pipeline (2021): Critical infrastructure ransomware incident
  • Equifax (2017): Data breach affecting 147 million people
